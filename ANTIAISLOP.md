==================================================
ANTI-AI-SLOP DESIGN DIRECTIVE
==================================================

This website must NOT look AI-generated.

Do not optimize for "wow" in the first 3 seconds.

Do not use visual trends simply because they are popular in modern AI-generated websites.

The design should feel like it was art-directed by a senior brand designer for a real professional advisory firm.

IMPORTANT:

Avoid the visual language of generic AI landing pages.

DO NOT USE:

- gradient blobs
- glowing gradients
- purple/blue AI gradients
- glassmorphism
- floating glass cards
- excessive rounded rectangles
- oversized pill buttons
- giant text covering half the screen
- abstract 3D blobs
- floating spheres
- mesh gradients
- random dots everywhere
- excessive grain
- fake dashboard screenshots
- generic financial dashboards
- generic stock photographs
- businessmen shaking hands
- people staring at laptops
- AI-generated office scenes
- floating charts for decoration
- meaningless animated numbers
- excessive parallax
- excessive scroll-triggered animations
- cursor-following effects
- magnetic buttons
- text scrambling effects
- typewriter effects
- marquee text everywhere
- excessive hover animations
- animated borders everywhere
- neon accents
- futuristic finance visuals
- "AI startup" aesthetics
- generic SaaS sections
- pricing-card layouts
- testimonial carousels
- fake logos
- fake client counts
- fake awards
- fake statistics
- meaningless badges
- "trusted by" sections without real evidence
- generic icon grids
- 20-card service grids
- excessive shadows
- excessive rounded corners
- unnecessary gradients
- excessive use of gold

If a design element does not communicate information,
remove it.

If an animation exists only because it looks impressive,
remove it.

If a card exists only because modern websites use cards,
remove it.

If a section could be removed without losing meaning,
remove it.

==================================================
VISUAL REFERENCE
==================================================

The website should feel closer to:

- a premium independent advisory firm
- a boutique professional services firm
- an established private advisory practice
- a high-end editorial business publication
- a well-designed annual report

It should NOT feel like:

- a startup landing page
- an AI company
- a Web3 company
- a SaaS company
- a template marketplace demo


==================================================
LAYOUT
==================================================

Use an editorial grid.

Do not make every section symmetrical.

Use:

- asymmetric columns
- large margins
- unexpected but intentional whitespace
- strong left alignment
- occasional full-width statements
- thin rules
- carefully placed gold accents
- varying section density

Let some areas breathe.

Do not fill every empty space.


==================================================
CARDS
==================================================

Use cards only when they improve information architecture.

Prefer:

- typography
- borders
- rules
- columns
- numbered sections
- editorial blocks

over:

"four rounded cards with an icon, heading and paragraph."


==================================================
COLOUR
==================================================

Use the Juristax navy as the dominant brand colour.

Use gold very sparingly.

Gold should feel like an accent used by a luxury print designer.

It should NOT look like:

"navy website + everything gold."

A good rule:

Most of the page should be navy, ivory and neutral.

Gold should appear in carefully selected moments.


==================================================
TYPOGRAPHY
==================================================

Typography should do most of the visual work.

Use a sophisticated serif for major statements.

Use a clean sans-serif for supporting information.

Do not use huge 120px typography simply to make the hero look impressive.

Large typography should be editorial, not theatrical.

Avoid:

"BUILD. GROW. SUCCEED."

"YOUR SUCCESS. OUR EXPERTISE."

"THE FUTURE OF BUSINESS."

These are generic marketing phrases.

Use actual meaning.


==================================================
IMAGERY
==================================================

Do not use generic stock photography.

If imagery is needed, prefer:

- architectural details
- documents
- refined financial objects
- close-up material textures
- office details
- subtle abstract brand graphics
- carefully art-directed compositions

However, imagery is optional.

A strong typography-led website is preferable to mediocre stock imagery.


==================================================
ANIMATION
==================================================

Animation should be almost invisible.

Use only:

- gentle opacity transitions
- very small translations
- restrained hover states
- subtle line movement

Most of the website should still feel excellent with animations disabled.

No animation should distract from reading.


==================================================
BRAND-SPECIFIC DESIGN
==================================================

The website must feel specifically designed for Juristax.

Use the actual JA logo as the source of visual inspiration.

The gold curved element in the logo can inspire:

- one or two section dividers
- a subtle line
- a directional graphic
- a small decorative motif

Do not repeat it everywhere.

The design should have its own visual grammar derived from the logo,
rather than simply placing the logo at the top of a generic template.


==================================================
CONTENT
==================================================

Write like a senior advisor speaking to a business owner.

Not like a marketing agency.

Not like an AI.

Not like a startup founder.

Not like a LinkedIn motivational post.

Every sentence should communicate something useful.

Avoid:

"Empowering businesses to unlock their full potential."

Instead:

"We help businesses make clearer decisions around tax, finance and compliance."

Avoid:

"Comprehensive solutions tailored to your unique needs."

Instead:

"From tax planning to regulatory compliance, we handle the areas that require careful attention."

Avoid:

"Your success is our priority."

Instead:

"Good advice should help you make a better decision."


==================================================
THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE
==================================================

When choosing between:

A) a visually impressive trendy component

and

B) a simple, beautifully typeset editorial layout

ALWAYS choose B.

The website should look expensive because of its restraint,
not because of effects.

The finished result should make someone think:

"This was designed."

Not:

"This was generated."
